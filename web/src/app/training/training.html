01

Training

Feedback here does not retrain a model — there is no model in the review path, and there is not going to be. What it does is move a named, versioned set of per-rule levers: which rules run, what severity they report at, which occupancies they govern. Every change is diffable, reversible, and attributable to the feedback that argued for it.

That is the trade. A trained model could learn a check nobody wrote; this cannot. In exchange, you can show a permit applicant exactly why a finding says what it says, which is the property this product is actually for.
