01

Refine the analysis

Nothing here retrains a model — there is no model in the review path, and there is not going to be. What your feedback moves is a named, versioned set of per-rule levers: which rules run, what severity they report at, which occupancies they govern. Every change is diffable, reversible, and attributable to the feedback that argued for it.

That is the trade. A trained model could learn a check nobody wrote; this cannot. In exchange, you can show a permit applicant exactly why a finding says what it says, which is the property this product is actually for.

Running one against your own levers

The checkbox on the upload form decides which profile a new review runs against: your own candidate, so you see the effect of feedback you have already given, rather than the approved one everybody else gets.
